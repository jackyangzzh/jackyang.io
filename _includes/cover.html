One orange dot carries it from the live light through Teams AI, Mesh, Holos and FakeHaul, and lands as the full stop on the site's headline. Source: _motion/full-stop. The video is decorative and carries its own accessible description; the caption beside it (below it on narrow screens) identifies the reel and its duration. `start` is where the first play opens, in seconds: Full Stop's first second is a lone dot on black, which reads as "not loaded" mid-scroll.
