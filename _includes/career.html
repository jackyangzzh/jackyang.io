{%- comment -%} The career at a glance: one lane each for work, research and study, a bar per role placed by date, and a red line at now. A role still running is red to the line; one that ends in the future is ink up to now and open after it. Each bar jumps to its row below. Bars that overlap in time stack onto up to three rows. Positions are computed here, so it renders without JavaScript. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign r = site.data.resume -%} {%- assign now = site.time | date: "%s" | plus: 0 -%} {%- assign all = r.work | concat: r.research | concat: r.education -%} {%- assign min_s = now -%}{%- assign max_e = now -%} {%- for it in all -%} {%- if it.startDate == nil or it.startDate == "" -%}{%- continue -%}{%- endif -%} {%- assign s = it.startDate | date: "%s" | plus: 0 -%} {%- if s < min_s -%}{%- assign min_s = s -%}{%- endif -%} {%- if it.endDate and it.endDate != "" -%}{%- assign e = it.endDate | date: "%s" | plus: 0 -%}{%- if e > max_e -%}{%- assign max_e = e -%}{%- endif -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign pad = 5184000 -%}{%- comment -%}60 days{%- endcomment -%} {%- assign t0 = min_s | minus: pad -%} {%- assign span = max_e | plus: pad | minus: t0 -%} {%- assign y0 = t0 | date: "%Y" | plus: 1 -%} {%- assign y1 = t0 | plus: span | date: "%Y" | plus: 0 -%} {%- assign now_l = now | minus: t0 | times: 100.0 | divided_by: span | round: 3 -%} {%- assign lanes = "Work,Research,Study" | split: "," -%}
