I build real-time AI agents at Microsoft Teams AI, where I work on Facilitator, the Microsoft 365 Copilot agent that runs inside live Teams meetings. My focus is the orchestration, tool execution, context management, and multimodal features that let an agent keep up with a conversation as it happens.

Before Teams AI, I was a key contributor to the avatar rendering pipelines and shared UI at Microsoft Mesh, which shipped weekly in 20+ languages to millions of users. Earlier, I built AR/VR interaction and multiplayer systems at Holos, a Techstars startup, and helped it secure a $750K U.S. Air Force contract.

Outside work, I created FakeHaul, an AI-generated marketplace where every checkout is $0.00, covered by The New York Times and Fast Company. I’m also completing an M.S. in HCI at Georgia Tech, with a paper under review at CHI 2027 on how AI systems that track decisions from transcripts can hide their own mistakes. I enjoy connecting low-latency systems with generative models, and I care as much about how the interaction feels as how the system is built.

Publications

  1. Under review CHI 2027 PDF

    Too Late to Rely: A Self-Concealing Failure in State-Only Transcript Grounding Trackers

    Transcript-based trackers that keep only the latest state can hide their own failures: if someone acts on a decision before the conversation supports it, later agreement overwrites the warning that they acted too early. Used a 264-dialogue benchmark to show the failure and test an event-sourced record that preserves those warnings.
