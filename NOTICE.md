The site's layouts, styles and scripts were written for jackyang.io. Earlier
versions were built on the Hydejack theme; none of its code, images or icons
ship with the current site.

## Typefaces

One family is self-hosted from `assets/fonts/` and used under the
[SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/).

| Typeface | Use | Copyright |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| [Archivo](https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo) | Display, text and interface | © 2020 The Archivo Project Authors |

The Latin subset comes from [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org/); the license
text sits beside the files in `assets/fonts/`.

The primitive-fitting diagram in the Holos case study embeds
[JetBrains Mono](https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono) and
[Public Sans](https://github.com/uswds/public-sans), also under the OFL; their license
texts sit beside the diagram in `assets/img/projects/holos/`.

## Icons

The interface icons are inline SVG drawn for this site. The GitHub, LinkedIn,
Medium and X marks belong to their respective owners and are used only to link
to those profiles.
